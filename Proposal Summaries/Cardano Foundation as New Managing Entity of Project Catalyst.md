**Title:** Approve Cardano Foundation as New Managing Entity of Project Catalyst

**Governance Action Type:** Info Action

**Submitted:** 17th Mar 2026 (Epoch 619)

**Expires:** 19th Apr 2026 (Epoch 626) 

**Status:** Active

**TLDR:** 
**Project Catalyst Stewardship Transition:** Project Catalyst governance transitions from IOG to the Cardano Foundation, ensuring continuity for over 500 active projects, pausing Fund15 and Fund16, and returning previously allocated ada to the treasury.

---

## 📌 Summary (What the Proposal Is)

This governance action addresses the future of Project Catalyst funding and management, proposing a shift in how Catalyst is administered — moving responsibility away from IOG (Input Output Global) and toward the Cardano Foundation (CF) (or another formalized structure).

At a high level, the proposal is about:
- Continuity of Catalyst funding
- Who operates and governs Catalyst going forward
- How treasury resources are allocated to community innovation

## 🧠 Gimbalabs Perspective

Gimbalabs has a strong positive bias toward Catalyst:

Catalyst has been a primary onboarding and experimentation engine for the ecosystem

Many builders (including Gimbalabs contributors) have learned, collaborated, and shipped projects through Catalyst

At the same time, we recognize ongoing tensions:

### ⚖️ Key Tension: Centralization vs Coordination

The transition from IOG → Cardano Foundation raises an important question:

Does this increase coordination… or reintroduce centralization?

Potential Benefits
- CF may provide stability, continuity, and institutional support
- Clear ownership could improve execution, accountability, and operations

Potential Risks
Concentrating control in a single entity may:

- Reduce community-driven evolution
- Limit experimentation and diversity of funding approaches
- Catalyst could drift from a community innovation lab toward a more top-down grant program

🧩 Governance Maturity Moment
This proposal reflects a broader shift:
Catalyst is moving from:
IOG-led governance and innovation funding experimentation

Toward:
formal governance structures (CF, DReps, Treasury controls)

Gimbalabs lens:

This is necessary for scale. But must be balanced with:
- permissionless participation
- community-led iteration
- transparent decision-making
